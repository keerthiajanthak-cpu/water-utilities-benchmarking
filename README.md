# Government-Owned Water Utilities: Financial & Performance Benchmarking 2024-25

An independent analysis of nine state and territory-owned major water utilities in Australia, assessing financial performance, returns to Government, financial risk and customer bills.

## Tools
Excel (SUMIFS, data cleaning, ratio analysis, charts)

## Data
Bureau of Meteorology, National Performance Report 2024-25: urban water utilities (dataset published 2026).
Utilities: Sydney Water, Hunter Water, SA Water, Water Corporation (Perth), Icon Water, Greater Western Water, South East Water, Yarra Valley Water, Barwon Water.

## What I did
1. Filtered 45,000+ rows to 9 government-owned utilities and 7 key indicators.
2. Built a summary table using SUMIFS and converted text values to numbers.
3. Calculated NPAT margin, dividend payout, net debt to equity, capex per property and bill vs peer median.
4. Identified and fixed a data quality issue: net debt to equity was reported as a percentage by some utilities and as a ratio by others.
5. Built a dashboard and wrote a one-page briefing note with findings, risks and recommendations.

## Key findings
- SA Water paid dividends equal to 106% of its net profit, the highest in the group.
- SA Water has the highest net debt to equity ratio (2.9x vs peer median 1.6x).
- SA Water's typical bill is 14% above the peer median.
- Icon Water shows the strongest balance of profitability and low debt.
- Water Corporation (Perth) data shows a 49% margin but no dividends, which needs verification.
